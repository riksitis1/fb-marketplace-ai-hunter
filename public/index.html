📖 How to get your Facebook cookies in 30 seconds
  1. Open Facebook.com in Chrome/Edge and ensure you are logged in.
  2. Press F12 (or right click → Inspect) to open Developer Tools.
  3. Click the Application tab (or Storage in Firefox) at the top.
  4. Under the left sidebar, expand Cookies → click https://www.facebook.com.
  5. Look for the values of c_user and xs. You can paste them as: c_user=YOUR_ID; xs=YOUR_XS_TOKEN;
  6. Alternatively, in the Network tab, refresh the page, click any request to facebook.com, look at Request Headers → right click Cookie → Copy value.

🔒 Privacy Guarantee: Your cookies are stored only in your private browser session and only sent to your backend server to query the Marketplace search endpoint. They are never shared or logged.
